Welcome to Digital Paathsaala

Digital Paathsaala is a free educational platform designed specifically for Class 6 and 10 (CBSE/STATE BOARD) students. Our goal is to break down complex topics and explain them in a simple, engaging, and interactive way.

Our Mission

We believe that everyone deserves access to quality education. Our mission is to provide students with easy-to-understand study materials, tests, and animated video lectures for subjects like Science, Mathematics, and Geography—absolutely free of cost.

What We Provide

  • Interactive Mock Tests: User-friendly online tests to evaluate your learning and prepare for exams.
  • Video Lectures: Detailed chapter explanations using animations and visual maps.
  • Study Notes: Clear, concise, and exam-oriented study materials.
